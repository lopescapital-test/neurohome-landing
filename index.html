What We Address

Does your child struggle with any of these?

Autism shows up differently in every child. NeuroHome looks beneath the diagnosis at the neurology driving these patterns, not just the surface behavior.

Speech Delays

Targeted protocols help children find their voice, building the neural foundation for expressive language.

Social Engagement

Eye contact, joint attention, and back-and-forth communication develop as the social brain reorganizes.

Sensory Processing

For children overwhelmed by sounds, lights, textures, or crowds, we work to calm the nervous system at the brainstem.

Meltdowns & Anxiety

Severe emotional dysregulation responds to vagal-tone work and primitive reflex integration.

Sleep Dysregulation

When sleep breaks down, everything does. We work to restore the circadian and brainstem inputs that govern it.

Feeding & Texture Aversion

We treat texture aversion and a limited food repertoire as sensory-motor problems to work on, not fixed traits.

We don't treat autism as a set of behaviors to be managed. We treat the nervous system underneath them: the immune, gut, and neurological patterns driving how your child feels and functions.

Support the biology, and behavior follows.

02The method

The same in-clinic sequence, at home.

The question we start with

What is driving your child's nervous system right now, and what input does it need every day to change?

01Look beneath

Look beneath the diagnosis

At-home lab kits go out before anything else. What comes back tells us which systems are actually under load: immune, inflammatory, gut, metabolic, nutritional, environmental.

Two children with the same label rarely have the same findings.

Next · 02 Regulate the nervous system
02Regulate

Regulate the nervous system

Red, green and near-infrared light, paired with supplements chosen from your child's labs. Run at home, on your schedule, monitored by your clinician.

A nervous system in alarm can't learn. Calm comes first.

Next · 03 Rehabilitate & consolidate
03Rehabilitate

Rehabilitate & consolidate

Once regulation holds, sensory-motor and developmental work builds on it (speech, communication, coordination, daily function), guided live by your clinician.

You get a daily plan, not a homework list.

Next · 04 Measure what changes
04Measure

Measure what changes

The ATEC is scored at baseline, week 4 and week 8 across the same four domains, so you and your clinician are reading the same picture at the same time.

You see what moved, not just how it felt.

That's the whole sequence: eight weeks, in your house.
