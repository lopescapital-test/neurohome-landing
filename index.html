The Method

Redefining Results Through

Progressive Methods

Most families come to Dr. Kyle after years of being told what their child has. His process starts with a different question:

What is driving your child's nervous system right now — and what input does it need every day to reorganize?
Step 01
We figure out what's actually driving your child's nervous system
Dr. Kyle doesn't treat every child as the same diagnosis. The first step is identifying the dominant clinical pattern driving the nervous system — foundational delay, sensory-motor, birth trauma, environmental, hyperimmune, regression, or severe dysregulation. Two children with the same label may receive very different plans.
Step 02
We start where your child can actually succeed today
The process is bottom-up. We begin with the systems that determine whether your child can even receive therapy: brainstem regulation, vagal tone, sensory safety, sleep, primitive reflexes, balance, coordination, and timing.
Step 03
We give you a daily plan, not a homework list
A predictable daily structure — a morning block, a main therapy session, midday touch-ups, and an evening wind-down. The plan tells parents what to do, when, and how much. No guessing.
Step 04
We adjust to your real life, not an ideal one
Some days are LIGHT, some MID, some HIGH. The plan flexes to your child's nervous system — not the other way around. The plan respects biology. It meets the nervous system where it is, then gradually builds capacity.
